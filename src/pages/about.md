I've spent my career working in marketing, data, and operations — across media
and tech.

Sometimes I get inspired and feel like I have something worth saying. Instead of
talking my coworkers' ears off, I turn that energy into writing.

Most of that writing lives on [Medium](https://medium.com/@davidmrguo): random
thoughts on data, business, and systems — with a touch of philosophy.

## What this site is for

This is the companion to those posts — a mini content site that accommodates the
material the posts refer to but can't comfortably hold.

A prompt worth reading in full usually runs longer than the post about it. The
same goes for a config, a spec, or a set of rules. Rather than bury them in a
scroll of code blocks, they get their own pages here: linkable, copy-pasteable,
and easy to keep current as they change.

The posts stay on Medium. The artifacts live here.
